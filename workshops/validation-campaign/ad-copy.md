## Creative 1: Static Image / Graphic

### Visual Concept
A clean, modern, split-image graphic:
* **Left side**: A high-quality photo of Veronica Pillay smiling professionally.
* **Right side**: A clean mockup of a modern house in Montreal/Laval or a bold, solid brand-colored background with high-contrast text:
  * *"STOP RENTING. START OWNING."* or *"L'IMMOBILIER SIMPLIFIÉ."*
