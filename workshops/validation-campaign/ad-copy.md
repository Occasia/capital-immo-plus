# Meta Ad Copy & Video Scripts (Capital Immo Community)

This document contains the copy and scripts for the **3 ad creatives** to build the waitlist for the **Capital Immo Community**.

---

## Creative 1: Static Image / Graphic

### Visual Concept
A clean, modern, split-image graphic:
* **Left side**: A high-quality photo of Veronica Pillay smiling professionally.
* **Right side**: A clean mockup of a modern house in Montreal/Laval or a bold, solid brand-colored background with high-contrast text:
  * *"STOP RENTING. START OWNING."* or *"L'IMMOBILIER SIMPLIFIÉ."*
* **Overlay Badge**: *Capital Immo Community - Free Workshops*

## Creative 2: B-Roll Video with Text Overlay

### Video Concept
* **Length**: 15–20 seconds
* **Visuals**: A compilation of high-quality vertical B-roll:
  * 0:00 - 0:05: Scenic drone shot of residential streets in Montreal or Laval (duplexes/triplexes).
  * 0:05 - 0:10: Close-up of hands typing numbers on a laptop or sketching on a notebook.
  * 0:10 - 0:15: Veronica Pillay in a modern meeting room talking/collaborating.
  * 0:15 - 0:20: A clean graphic transition showing: *Capital Immo Community (Waitlist Open)*.
* **Music**: Smooth, modern, low-fi or high-energy tech-business beat.
