# The Community Launch Strategy & Execution Plan

**Brand:** Capital Immo Plus (Veronica Pillay)  
**Community Name:** Capital Immo Community  
**Primary Goal:** Collect high-quality waitlist leads (Renters, First-Time Buyers, and Aspiring Investors in Montreal & Laval) using Meta Ads.

---

## Strategy Focus & Traffic Acquisition (Pillar A & B)

Since Veronica does not have an established organic audience or email database, **Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram)** is our single traffic engine. 

We have organized the rollout into two initial phases. **Phase 1 (Foundation)** is complete, and we are currently executing **Phase 2 (Validation/Smoke Test)**. Phase 3 (The 15-Day Launch Cycle) is removed from this execution plan for now and will be introduced later once validation is complete.

---

## Phase 1: The Foundation (Strategy & Brand) - COMPLETED

Before launching campaigns, we defined the core strategy, naming, and technical stack.

### 1. Define the Core Strategy
* **The Problem**:
  * **Pillar A**: Renters want to buy but are intimidated by high mortgage interest rates, down payments, and complex Quebec real estate laws.
  * **Pillar B**: Aspiring investors want to start but are afraid of making costly mistakes, overpaying, or failing landlord-tenant laws (TAL).
* **The Target Audience**:
  * **Pillar A**: Renters/tenants aged 25–45 in Montreal, Laval, and surrounding areas.
  * **Pillar B**: Salaried professionals or business owners in Laval/Montreal looking to diversify into real estate.
* **The Differentiation (The Vibe)**:
  * In-person, welcoming, action-oriented workshops. No hype or high-pressure sales pitches—just practical frameworks, local case studies, and real investment numbers.

### 2. Brand Identity & Landing Page
* **Naming**: The community is officially named **Capital Immo Community**.
* **Visuals**: Clean, high-trust, professional real estate branding.
* **The Asset**: High-converting registration pages built on Occasia, Eventbrite, or a dedicated website funnel, optimized for mobile lead capture (Name, Email, Phone Number).

### 3. Tech Stack & Automation
* **The Engine**: Meta Ads Manager + GoHighLevel/Klaviyo for automation and lead tracking.
* **The Welcome Flow**:
  * Immediate confirmation email/SMS with the event calendar invite.
  * Value-add resource (e.g., *Quebec Property Analysis Sheet*) sent automatically.
* **Testing**: Verification of pixel tracking, lead forms, and email/SMS triggers.

---

## Phase 2: The Validation (Smoke Test Campaign) - ACTIVE

We do not book venues or finalize event dates until we have validated lead demand. The sole goal of this phase is to collect leads for the waitlist at an optimal Cost Per Lead (CPL).

### 1. Funnel Design
* **Traffic Source**: Meta Ads targeting Laval/Montreal (+15km) looking at real estate interests (Zillow, Realtor.com, mortgage, property investing) or demographic filters (renters, young professionals aged 25–45).
* **Destination**: A high-converting waitlist landing page (or Meta Instant Form) collecting Name, Email, and Phone Number.
* **Thank You Page / Confirmation**: Immediate message confirming they are on the waitlist for the first free workshop and will be contacted via Email/SMS with venue details and dates.

### 2. Campaign Targets & Metrics
* **Lead Goal**: 200–400 waitlist leads.
* **Target Cost Per Lead (CPL)**: <$4.00 CAD (Benchmark: Aiming for $1.50–$2.50 CAD based on previous community validation runs).

### 3. The Creative Mix (4 Ads)

To optimize cost and test different hooks, we will launch 4 distinct ad creatives:

#### Ad 1: Static Image / Graphic
* **Format**: Clean, high-contrast, premium single-image ad.
* **Hook**: Direct and benefit-driven. E.g., *"Stop Renting, Start Owning. Join the Capital Immo Community waitlist for free in-person workshops in Montreal & Laval."*
* **Visual**: Clean real estate background or a professional photo of Veronica with text overlay.

#### Ad 2: B-Roll Video with Text Overlay
* **Format**: 15–30 second short-form video (Reel format) showing B-roll of local properties, Montreal/Laval streets, or someone calculating numbers.
* **Hook**: Emotional hook highlighting the struggle. E.g., Text overlay: *"Rent is going up... but buying feels impossible? We are building a community to change that. Join the Capital Immo Community."*

#### Ad 3: Facecam Invite Video - Variation A (Focus: Pillar A - First-Time Buyers)
* **Format**: 30–60 second vertical video with Veronica looking directly into the camera.
* **Hook**: Relatable and solution-oriented for first-time buyers. 
* **Script Focus**: *"I'm building a room for people in Montreal/Laval who want to buy their first home but don't know where to start with interest rates. Join our free community waitlist..."*

#### Ad 4: Facecam Invite Video - Variation B (Focus: Pillar B - Aspiring Investors)
* **Format**: 30–60 second vertical video with Veronica looking directly into the camera.
* **Hook**: Growth and wealth-building focused for new investors.
* **Script Focus**: *"Want to buy your first duplex or triplex in Quebec but afraid of making a costly mistake? I'm hosting free workshops for the Capital Immo Community. Join the waitlist..."*
